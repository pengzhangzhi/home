description: "My advice for doing ML research together, from making time for it to sharing what you learn when an experiment fails."
---

- **Be committed and spend time on it.** If you don't plan to spend enough time on research over the next few months, you won't get much out of it. If you do, I guarantee you'll get at least a paper out of it.
- **Be communicative.** Share interesting papers, random ideas, questions, and unexpected results. Let's brainstorm often.
- **Meet weekly.** Discuss progress, findings, challenges, and next steps.
- **Don't just tell me something doesn't work.** Tell me what you tried, what you observed, why you think it failed, and what we should try next. Your insights matter.
  - Please don't send me AI slop you haven't read yourself.
- **Enjoy it.** Science is a hobby. Enjoy discovering the unknown.
