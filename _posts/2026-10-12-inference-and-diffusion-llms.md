## Diffusion LLM startups

- [Inception Labs](https://www.inceptionlabs.ai/blog/introducing-mercury-2-5) — Mercury diffusion LLMs for reasoning, coding, and low-latency applications.


## Inference startups
- [PrismML](https://prismml.com/) — Bonsai low-bit models for efficient on-device inference.
- [Inco AI](https://inco.ai/blog/dflash2/) — DFlash team.
- [Mirai](https://trymirai.com/) — On-device inference, Apple Silicon models, uzu inference engine, and quantization.
- [diffuspace](https://diffuspace.ai/news) - on-device dLLM for  AI PCs, robotics, intelligent vehicles, and smart homes.

## dLLM

- [DiffusionGemma Technical Report](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.00146) — Google DeepMind’s open-weight discrete diffusion model.

## Continuous diffusion LLMs

- [Plaid: Likelihood-Based Diffusion Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.18619)
- [RePlaid](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.18530)
- [Sigma: Large Language Continuous Diffusion Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.02665)
- [Clock Diffusion: Efficient Semi-Autoregressive Continuous Diffusion Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.00894) — builds on RePlaid’s training framework.
